02 / Case study

How a lead pipeline gets built

A look at my process on the lead capture pipeline, from problem to delivery.

Challenge

Enquiries arrived from different places and were easy to lose. Replies were slow and follow-up was manual.

Process

  1. Map the journeyWhere leads come from and what happens next.
  2. Build the landing pageOne clear page with a short, focused form.
  3. Automate with n8n and PythonCapture, validate and organise every lead.
  4. Test and refineRun real submissions end to end before launch.

Outcome

Every lead lands in one organised place and gets an instant reply, so nothing slips through and follow-up starts right away.
